FAQ

Questions, answered.

Who is JE Insights built for? +

Founders, operators, and investors who need rigorous thinking without standing up a full in-house research function. Most of our work is for early- and growth-stage teams making a consequential decision.

How fast can you turn around a sprint? +

Focused sprints typically run 2–3 weeks end-to-end. For time-sensitive questions we can produce a tactical brief in 72 hours.

What does a deliverable look like? +

A tight executive brief (5–15 pages), the supporting evidence and sources, and a recommended next move. We optimize for clarity, not page count.

Do you sign NDAs? +

Yes. Mutual NDAs are standard, and we’re happy to work under your paper.
